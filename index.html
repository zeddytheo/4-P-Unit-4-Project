Recipe #1 : Clear Cake

  • Time: 7 minutes
  • Serves: 1-2
Ingredients Quantity
Sprite 1 Can
Gelatin 100 grams
Love ∞

Step 1: Mix the 100 grams of gelatin with 1 can of Sprite in a bowl. Stir until the gelatin is completely dissolved.

Step 2: Pour the mixture into a mold and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.

Step 3: Once set, remove the cake from the mold and serve. Enjoy your clear cake!

Recipe #2 : Fufu and Egusi

  • Time: 30 minutes
  • Serves: 4
Ingredients Quantity
Cassava flour 4 Cups
Palm oil Household bottle
Cooked Meat A bowl
Parsley 5 leaves
Seasoning of choices as many as you want
Love ∞

Step 1:In a clean pot, add a moderate amount of water and a little amount of palm oil or ground oil and allow it to boil for about 5 minutes.

Step 2: Sieve the cassava flour and add it into the boiling water and stir using a wooden paddle. continue to stir it until it’s stick and stacked up together and put aside. Package and arrange it in a cooler and set aside

Step 3: In a clean pot, add some palm oil into the pot and allow to fry.chop the onions and mix it the egusi and add a little amount of water to make a paste. Add the mixture into the the pot and stir on a low heat.

Step 4: After stirring for about 3-5 min, add some amount of stock meat into the pot and allow to cook for 3 min

Step 5:Wash and Cut the ugu leaves into smaller pieces, grind or blend the scotch bonnets, onions and bell peppers and set aside

Step 6:Add the blended bell peppers into the pot. Add crayfish, ugu leaves, cooked meat,kidney and liver, and fish into the pot

Step 7:Add your seasoning, spices (parsley is one of the spices) and a bit of salt into the pot and allow to cook for about 5-10Min

Step 8:Serve the egusi soup with the fufu and enjoy your meal!
